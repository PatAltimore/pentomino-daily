How to play

  • The picture of the day has been cut into pentomino pieces. Rebuild it on the board.
  • Drag a piece onto the board. Tap a piece to select it, tap again to rotate, double-tap to flip.
  • Use Rotate and Flip for the selected piece (keys: R and F on a keyboard).
  • A piece snaps to the grid. It only counts when it sits in the right spot, facing the right way.
  • Hint shows where one piece belongs and adds 15 seconds to your time.
  • Everyone gets the same puzzle each day.
