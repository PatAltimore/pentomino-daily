How to play

  • A picture of the day has been cut into pentomino pieces. Rebuild it on the board.
  • Drag a piece onto the board. Tap a piece to select it, tap again to rotate.
  • In this puzzle the pieces can't be rotated or flipped. They already face the right way, so just drag each one to its place.
  • Use the ⟲ counter-clockwise and ⟳ clockwise buttons to turn the selected piece, and Flip to mirror it (keys: R turns clockwise, Shift+R counter-clockwise, F flips).
  • Rebuilding the picture as a mirror image (left-to-right or top-to-bottom) also counts. A note is added to your time.
  • A piece snaps to the grid. It only counts when it sits in the right spot, facing the right way.
  • Hint shows where one piece belongs and adds 15 seconds to your time.
  • There are four puzzles a day, from easy to challenging. Everyone gets the same ones.
